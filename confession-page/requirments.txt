DATABASE SCHEMA:

Confession:
- text (String, required)
- secretCode (String, hashed using bcrypt)
- reactions (Object: like, love, laugh counts)
- createdAt (Date)
- userId (Google account ID)
- expiryDate (Date, optional)
- sentiment (String: Sad | Angry | Romantic | Funny | Regret | Neutral)

Reply:
- confessionId (ObjectId ref Confession)
- text (String)
- secretCode (hashed)
- createdAt (Date)
